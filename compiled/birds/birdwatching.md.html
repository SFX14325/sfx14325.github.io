Birdwatching

Birdwatching is the practice of observing wild birds and learning to recognize them by appearance, behavior, calls, and habitat.

A simple observation routine

  1. Pause and listen before moving.
  2. Look for movement in trees, shrubs, water, and open ground.
  3. Note the bird's size, shape, colors, and behavior.
  4. Record the location, date, weather, and habitat.
  5. Compare observations with Northern Cardinal and Black Capped Chickadee.

Useful equipment

  • Binoculars
  • A small field notebook
  • A bird identification guide
  • A camera or phone for reference photographs
