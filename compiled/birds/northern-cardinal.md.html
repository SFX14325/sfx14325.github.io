Northern Cardinal

The Northern Cardinal is a familiar songbird in the eastern and central United States. It is often seen in gardens, woodland edges, and neighborhoods.

Identification

  • Shape: Stout body, long tail, and prominent crest.
  • Male: Bright red overall with a dark face around the bill.
  • Female: Warm tan or brown with reddish accents in the wings, crest, and tail.
  • Bill: Thick, cone-shaped, and orange-red.

Behavior

Cardinals commonly visit feeders for seeds. They often forage in pairs or small family groups and may sing from exposed branches.
