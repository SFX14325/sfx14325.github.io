Bald Eagle

The Bald Eagle is a large bird of prey associated with lakes, rivers, and coastal areas. It is the national bird of the United States.

Identification

Adults have a dark brown body, white head and tail, and a large yellow bill. Immature birds are mostly brown and take several years to develop adult plumage.

Feeding and habitat

Bald Eagles primarily eat fish when available, but they also take waterbirds and carrion. They often nest in large trees near water.

Connections

Eagles are one example of a raptor. Their habitat needs connect to Backyard Habitat, although their territories are much larger than a typical backyard.
