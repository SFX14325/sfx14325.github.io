Blue Jay

The Blue Jay is a conspicuous songbird known for its blue, white, and black plumage, crest, and loud calls.

Identification

  • Blue upperparts with black barring on the wings and tail.
  • White underside and a black necklace across the throat.
  • A noticeable crest that can rise or flatten.

Behavior

Blue Jays eat nuts, seeds, insects, and other food. They can carry and cache acorns, helping move oak seeds away from parent trees.

Their calls are varied and often carry over long distances. They may imitate the calls of other birds.

Connections

Blue Jays and Northern Cardinal may both visit backyard feeders. Their food choices connect to Backyard Habitat.
