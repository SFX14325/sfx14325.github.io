Backyard Bird Habitat

A bird-friendly yard offers food, water, shelter, and places to raise young. Native plants are especially useful because they support local insects and produce seeds, berries, and cover.

Habitat elements

  • Native plants: Provide food and structure throughout the year.
  • Water: A clean, shallow bird bath can attract visitors.
  • Shelter: Dense shrubs and evergreen cover provide refuge.
  • Food: If using feeders, clean them regularly and place them where birds have nearby cover but are protected from window collisions.

Visitors to look for

You may see Northern Cardinal, Blue Jay, and Black Capped Chickadee.

Observation question

Which habitat feature seems to attract the greatest variety of birds in your area?
