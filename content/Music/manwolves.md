# Manwolves
Manwolves is band I listened too during early high-school and found I believe in middle school. It is one of the most peak jazz along with rap style musical artists I enjoy. They are distinct in nature and unfortunately had the standard artist fall off with the most peak album a few years back then disappeared for 4 years and came back and were not the same.
