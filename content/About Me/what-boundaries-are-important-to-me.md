# What boundaries are important to me?

## Boundaries
The idea of setting a comfortable limit or extent to which something becomes less comfortable.
## What does it mean to me?
To me, I have little to no boundaries I find, as long as you are willing to speak about or do something within the bounds of my moral compass. I am willing to participate in that discussion. I find that I try to be an open book present myself honestly of which I fail at in the attempt to remain without argue. Additionally, whatever you wish to know about me, I will tell with my whole heart to my greatest ability that won't cause disaster.
