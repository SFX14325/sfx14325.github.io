# What would make my life meaningful?
I think that what would make me believe that my life amounted to something worth meaning is that I had the opportunity to live it. Not that you accomplished anything, but that I had the opportunity to be here. That life is inherently meaningless, and the only meaning is generated from yourself and what you believe creates that meaning. So for me even being alive has created a meaning in its self. That does not mean I don't have things I would like to accomplish and dreams to be had, but that even getting to live has brought a meaning to living itself. And I think you should hold yourself to the same standard that you are free to do whatever you wish, but you should still recognize your own value and even if you feel like there has been no meaning to it all, remember the only meaning there is. Is the one you assign to it.
