# Receiving Gifts

There are among many things I dislike and receiving gifts just so happens to be one of them. The exact reason I could not be certain, but for me, I fell that gifts make me feel guilty as I know myself and I know that I reciprocate no emotion in return and offer no such value to the giver. Furthermore, when attempting to push people away it is harder to do so when you feel guilty for betraying them after all they did for you.
