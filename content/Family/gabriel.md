# Gabriel

### My Older Brother Gabriel

### What does he look like?
He is 6' 2"...? 210 pounds? I think I just know he is very tall has glasses and the same dirty blond / light brown hair most of us do, with blue eyes.

### Characteristics
He is someone who is dedicated to what he enjoys has been playing paintball every single Sunday since he was in high-school. Along with use to play Counter Strike and would play in our Old House we had as kids in the tiny little interest room so he could have Ethernet. He got a Computer science degree and "enjoys" his job I think and has always had a thing for computers in general sparking my enjoyment of them as well.

### Memorable items

Along with always being there if I ever did need him. Way back he bought minecraft in Beta it was insane to think that I got to see the most popular game of all time before most, because my brother had discovered it on newgrounds. He drove this Black SS Chevy cobalt with manual shift as his first car.
