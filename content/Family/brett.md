# Papa Brett

### My father Brett 
Has always been someone I believe I can look up too, although I believe he has many of problems of his own which I wish we would be less alike in having the sentiment that our problems are our own and to not be a shared problem for others.

### What does he look like?
He is about my height 5' 7" and 200 pounds of pure macho with ocean blue eyes and dark brown hair (more gray now but nevertheless), has two distinct tattoos on his calfs for both his parents of their home countries flags along with all me and my siblings birthdays.

### Characteristics
He is one of the hardest working people I know. Who obviously is undiagnosed ADHD or at least it feels like that at times. He has had the time and enjoyed, gardening with a semi-large garden, beekeeping, having a hot air balloon and pilot license along with being a real estate owner and a full-time home builder and bar owner. That being said he is hard working, but is one of the most sentimental people I know, he tries to hide it, but my [[About Me/family|Family]] can easily tell that he cares more than he lets on about us all.

### Memorable Items
When my [[About Me/family|Family]] was younger he would always be sure to everyday tell us that he loved us. And would say it in the same way every time. *Did I tell you I love you today?*. What a core memory.
