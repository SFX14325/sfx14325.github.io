# Diet Maxing

## Description
What do I mean by "Diet Maxing" you may ask I refer to eat as cheaply as possible while hitting all needed nutrient targets?

## In Practice
What does this look like? For me, I eat, lentils, chicken, broccoli, milk, eggs and occasionally bell pepper then supplement this with electrolytes flavor packets and a daily multi vitamin to do the best to maintain all needed nutrients while being in budget. Now is this tasty what's so ever no, but it does mean I feel great and don't have so much stomach pain like I regularly would.
