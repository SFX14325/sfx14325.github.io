# Black-capped Chickadee

A small, active songbird common across much of the northern United States and Canada.

## Identification

- Black cap and bib.
- White cheeks.
- Gray wings and back.
- Buff-colored sides.

## Behavior

Chickadees move actively through branches, often hanging upside down while searching for insects and seeds. They may visit feeders and take one seed at a time.

Their familiar call sounds like *chick-a-dee-dee-dee*. The number of dee notes can vary with context.
