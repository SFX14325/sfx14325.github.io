# Bird Migration

Bird migration is the regular seasonal movement of birds between breeding and non-breeding areas. The timing and distance vary by species.

## Why birds migrate

Migration can help birds reach areas with seasonal food supplies and suitable breeding conditions. Day length, weather, food availability, and inherited behavior can all influence timing.

## What to observe

- Changes in which species appear in your area.
- Large movements during favorable weather.
- Birds feeding heavily before departure.
- Differences between spring and fall migration.
